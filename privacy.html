Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how That Random Agency ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Micatch mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: when you create an account, we collect your email address and an authentication identifier through our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Profile and content: any data you create or save in the App (e.g., entries, preferences, in-app settings).
  • Purchase information: when you make an in-app purchase or start a subscription, we receive a transaction identifier and product identifier from Apple or Google. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Device and diagnostic data: device model, operating system version, app version, language, crash logs, and basic usage events.
  • Advertising identifiers: if you allow tracking on iOS (App Tracking Transparency) or do not opt out on Android, advertising SDKs may receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID).

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and improve the App.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Process in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Display ads (including non-personalized ads where applicable).
  • Diagnose crashes and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may process information about you. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — authentication and backend storage (supabase.com/privacy).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (revenuecat.com/privacy).
  • Google AdMob — advertising (policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — distribution and payments (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Expo — application runtime and updates (expo.dev/privacy).

4. Advertising

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Settings › Google › Ads). Where required, we will request your consent before personalized ads are shown.

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.
